# Privacy Policy for 10.00

**Effective Date: September 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **10.00** app ("the App") handles user information.

## 1. Information We Collect

10.00 does **not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or device information**.

The App does not require an account, login, or registration.

The App does not collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location information
- Contacts
- Photos or media
- Advertising identifiers
- Device identifiers
- Health or fitness information
- Payment information
- Other personal information

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores a small amount of game-related information locally on your Wear OS device using Android's local DataStore storage.

This information includes:

- Your top 10 best game times
- Haptic feedback preference
- Color hint preference

This information is stored **only on your device** and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

The stored game times are not associated with your name, account, email address, or any other personal identifier.

## 3. Information Processed During Gameplay

During gameplay, the App uses the device's monotonic system clock to measure elapsed time and determine your result.

The elapsed time is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted or uploaded anywhere.

The App also uses the device's vibration functionality to provide haptic feedback during gameplay.

## 4. Internet and Network Access

10.00 does not require an internet connection to function.

The App does not request internet access and does not communicate with external servers, APIs, or online services.

No personal information or game data is transmitted from the App.

## 5. Third-Party Services

10.00 does not currently use third-party services that collect or process user information.

The App does not include:

- Advertising services
- Analytics services
- Crash reporting services
- Social media services
- User account services
- Cloud storage or synchronization
- External APIs

## 6. Data Sharing

10.00 does not sell, share, or disclose user information to third parties.

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no user data available to be shared with the developer or third-party services.

## 7. Data Security

All game-related information stored by the App remains on the user's device and is protected by the security mechanisms provided by the Wear OS and Android operating systems.

The developer does not have access to the locally stored game data.

## 8. Children's Privacy

10.00 does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

The App does not require users to provide personal information in order to play the game.

## 9. Data Deletion

Because all game data is stored locally on the user's device, users can remove the App and its locally stored data through the device's standard app management or uninstall process.

The developer does not maintain a copy of the user's game data.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the App's functionality or data practices change.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
